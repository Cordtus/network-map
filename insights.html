How nodes are identified

Networks are crawled from public RPC endpoints and P2P/PEX peer gossip; every discovered host is geolocated and enriched with provider/ASN and WHOIS data.

This is not necessarily a complete list of all nodes for a given network — some nodes may be specifically configured not to announce their existence to the rest of the network.
